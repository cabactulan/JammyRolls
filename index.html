The Art of JammyRolls

How A JammyRoll Is Born

We combined the irresistible crunch of Spanish churros with the beloved warmth of Philippine banana rolls and decadent chocolate fillings.

1️⃣

Hand-Rolled with Love

Fresh pastry dough rolled around sweet bananas and premium fillings, sealed to lock in all the molten goodness.

2️⃣

Flash Fried at 375°F

Crisped in hot vegetable oil until blistered, golden brown, and light as air, keeping the core warm and gooey.

3️⃣

Cinnamon Sugar & Dip

Dusted straight from the fryer with raw cane sugar and organic cinnamon, paired with our velvety dips.
