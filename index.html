About

Our Recipe

The crunch of Spanish churros combined with sweet Philippine bananas and rich chocolate lava.

1️⃣

Hand-Rolled with Love

Fresh pastry dough rolled around sweet bananas and premium fillings, sealed to lock in all the molten goodness.

Flash Fried at 375°F
